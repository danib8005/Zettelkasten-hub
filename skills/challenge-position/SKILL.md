# Challenge a position

Follow `AGENTS.md` and your agent file. Tool-neutral: the position comes as text, thesis or note path.

## Procedure
1. **State the position** in one or two sentences and confirm it with the person. Say what it is used for (talk, text, recommendation).
2. **Check its inner consistency:** gaps in the argument, circular reasoning, undefined terms, silent assumptions.
3. **Search the notes for counter-evidence:** sources that see it differently, limits and conditions under which it fails, places where theory and practice differ. Name every note path. If the notes hold no counter-position, say «not in the notes» and do not invent one.
4. **Rank the objections** from strongest to weakest. For each: what a critic would say, what in the notes supports it, what would answer it.
5. **Offer, never decide.** Mark objections as hypotheses. The person judges «holds» or «does not hold». Offer to save their own revised thinking as a thought note (hand over to Capture) and open questions (`record-open-question`, after consent).

## Final check
- Every objection names its origin in the notes or is marked «not from the notes».
- The person's position was not rewritten; nothing saved without consent.
