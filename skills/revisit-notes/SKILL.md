# Revisit notes

Follow `AGENTS.md` and your agent file. Tool-neutral.

## Procedure
1. **Pick the notes.** Only processed notes (concepts, frameworks, syntheses, source notes with a review status), never `00_Inbox`. Prefer notes with an old last review date or a ⚠️ mark; vary the folders.
2. **Show each note briefly:** title, core statement, path. No retelling of the whole note.
3. **Ask one question per note** that needs the owner's thinking: where does this hold today, what has changed since the review, what contradicts it. Questions stay in the chat; they do not go into the note.
4. **Take the answers.** If the person has a new thought, hand it to Capture as a thought note in their wording. If a note needs updating, hand it to Maintain (mark ⚠️) or Connect (rework).

## Final check
- Only processed notes were used; no raw captures.
- Questions stayed in the chat; the owner's words were not changed.
