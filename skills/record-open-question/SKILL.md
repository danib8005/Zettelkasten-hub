# Record an open question

Follow `AGENTS.md` and your agent file. Tool-neutral: without file access, show the note in the chat for the person to save.

## Procedure
1. **Offer, do not decide.** After the gap list of an answer, ask once whether one of the gaps should be kept as an open question. Without a clear yes, stop here.
2. **Check what exists.** Search `50_Open-Questions/` for the same or a similar question; if it exists, propose adding the new occasion to it instead.
3. **Fill `templates/open-question.md`.** The question, phrased as a question; where it came from (the question asked, the date); what the notes already say, with links; what is missing; a possible next step (capture a source, distill, connect, ask someone).
4. **Save** as `50_Open-Questions/<question-in-short>.md`, lowercase-with-hyphens, review status ❓ open, signed with your tag.
5. **Report** the path and suggest Maintain, which turns open questions into the next inputs.

## Final check
- Saved only after the person said yes.
- Only `50_Open-Questions/` was written; the answer's gaps were not presented as findings.
