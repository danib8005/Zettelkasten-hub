# Discover connections

Follow `AGENTS.md` and your agent file. Tool-neutral. Not to be confused with the Connect phase, which writes concepts and syntheses.

## Procedure
1. **Read the verdicts first.** Open `50_Open-Questions/connection-verdicts.md` if it exists; never propose a pair marked rejected. Create the file from `templates/open-question.md` when the first verdict is saved.
2. **Set the scope:** a topic, a note, or the whole bundle. Let the material speak before attaching it to favourite topics.
3. **Search beyond the obvious:** notes from different folders or authors that share a mechanism, tensions between sources, notes with fewer than two outgoing links, concepts that could join a synthesis. Prefer surprising but defensible pairs.
4. **Present each pair as a hypothesis:** the two notes (paths), the shared point or the tension in one sentence, why it might matter. Mark it «hypothesis».
5. **Take the verdict:** «fits» or «does not fit», with the reason in the person's words. Record it in the verdicts file (pair, verdict, reason, date). «Fits» goes on to Connect (a link with a reason, or a concept or synthesis).

## Final check
- No rejected pair proposed again; every pair marked as hypothesis.
- Verdicts recorded in the person's words; no link added without confirmation.
