# Inspire

Follow `AGENTS.md` and your agent file. Tool-neutral: works in any agent or chat tool; without file access, ask the person to paste the notes or the topic. Not to be confused with `discover-connections` (pairs of notes as hypotheses) or `map-frontier` (where the notes end): an impulse is an invitation to look, read, ask, try or pause, not an answer.

## Principles
- **A pointer, not a verdict.** The reply may be a hint to see, read, ask or do something. A full answer is not the goal; a next thought is.
- **Small.** One to three impulses per call, never more. Each fits in four lines.
- **Honest.** Everything from the notes names its path. Everything from outside is labelled «not from the notes» and a hypothesis. Never invent authors, titles, years, quotes or locations.
- **Optional.** An impulse never nags: no counters, no reminders, no "you have not looked at X for N days".

## Ways to an impulse
Pick one or two per call and vary them from the last time.

| Way | What it offers |
|---|---|
| Return | An older note, thought or question to read again, in the light of something newer |
| Collide | Two distant notes held next to each other |
| Contradict | A tension between two sources or with the owner's own position |
| Invert | An assumption from the notes turned around, or a claim reduced by taking something away |
| Future | A ten-year picture, a weak signal, a «what if this became normal?» |
| Outside | Something beyond the notes: a person to meet, an article or book to read, a place or catalogue to search, something to watch |
| Act | A small test, a sketch, a demo instead of more thinking |
| Pause | Walk away, sleep on it, leave it unprocessed on purpose |

## Procedure
1. **Read the log first.** Open `50_Open-Questions/impulse-log.md` if it exists: never repeat an impulse marked «does not fit», and vary from the last ones. Read `50_Open-Questions/connection-verdicts.md` too: rejected pairs stay rejected. Create the log from `templates/open-question.md` when the first impulse is saved.
2. **Set the start.** A topic, a question, a project, or nothing:
   - With a topic: notes on it, and the notes around its edges.
   - With nothing: pick a thread from `00_Inbox` (raw thoughts, questions, captures), thought notes in `10_Concepts`, open questions in `50_Open-Questions`, or notes with an old or ⚠️ review. Say which of these are raw and which are processed.
3. **Choose the ways.** One or two from the table; at least one impulse should be a surprise the person would not have asked for.
4. **Build each impulse in three lines:**
   - **From:** the note path(s) that triggered it, or «not from the notes».
   - **Do:** one action in one verb: read, see, ask, try, write, walk away.
   - **Why:** one sentence, marked «hypothesis».
5. **Outside the notes.** Use a search tool if one is available; otherwise give the kind of source and search terms instead of titles.
   - Meeting: a kind of person or role and a question to bring, not a name unless the person gave it.
   - Reading: cite only what a search returned or the person supplied; add the link.
   - **An article or text the person supplies** may be analysed as an impulse: what it adds to the notes, where it meets or contradicts a note (path), what it does not say. Label the analysis «not from the notes» for everything beyond the text.
   - Catalogues and databases: name the type and the query, e.g. «a library catalogue, search: …», and let the person run it.
6. **Ask for the verdict:** «fits», «does not fit», or «tried». Take the reason in the person's words.
7. **Record** in `50_Open-Questions/impulse-log.md`: date, way, the impulse in one line, verdict, reason. Only «does not fit» and «tried» are kept as lasting entries; «fits» goes on to the handoff.
8. **Hand over.** A new source or question → Capture; a confirmed connection → Connect (a link with a reason, a concept, a thought note in the person's words); a position to test → Reflect.

## Unattended runs (routines)
When a routine starts this skill, nobody answers questions. Produce one to three impulses, append them to `impulse-log.md` with the verdict «unrated» and the routine's name, and write nothing else. The person rates them when they read the log. A routine never saves notes, links or open questions.

## Final check
- One to three impulses, each with an action and a hypothesis label; paths for everything from the notes.
- Everything outside the notes labelled «not from the notes»; nothing invented; raw and processed material told apart.
- No rejected impulse or pair repeated; verdicts in the person's words; nothing saved beyond `impulse-log.md` without consent.
