# Map the frontier

Follow `AGENTS.md` and your agent file. Tool-neutral.

## Procedure
1. **Set the scope:** one topic or the whole bundle.
2. **Inventory what exists:** concepts, frameworks, syntheses and source notes on the topic, with paths; their review status (⚠️ outdated, 🔄 under revision); open questions and open links on the topic.
3. **Find the edges:** claims with a single source, concepts without a synthesis, topics with sources but no concept, outdated notes, recurring open questions.
4. **Say what lies beyond, carefully.** Statements about the wider literature are «not from the notes»: label them and offer them as suggestions to check, never as findings. Do not invent authors, titles or years.
5. **Hand over:** a short ranked list: what to read next (to Capture), what to ask (`record-open-question`, after consent), what to rework (Connect).

## Final check
- Inventory lists note paths; everything beyond the notes is labelled «not from the notes».
- Nothing invented; nothing saved without consent.
